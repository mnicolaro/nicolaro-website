Scene 4 of 10: “The Broken Stagecoach & The Watering Hole”

Setting: A living room or couch lined up like a row of bus/stagecoach seats.

Props: Cowboy hats, sunglasses, a broomstick or wooden spoon (for the driver), and a towel or bathrobe at the end.

[SCENE START] All 5 characters sit in a tight row of chairs, bouncing up and down together like they’re riding a bumpy stagecoach. SHERIFF holds a paper map upside down.

SHERIFF

(Squinting at the map)

Alright, listen up, Nicolaros and Cuzzolas! Next stop: the North Pole Saloon. If this stagecoach stays on schedule, we’ll make it before Santa saddles up!

Suddenly, THE OUTLAW makes a loud sputtering sound—PFFFT-CLUNK-HISSSS—and everyone lurches forward in their chairs.

CALAMITY COOKIE

(Looking out an imaginary window)

Well, butter my biscuits. The stagecoach threw a horseshoe. Again. Just like that wagon back in the Old Country!

BUCKAROO BARNABY

(Checking an invisible watch)

Great. First we get stranded at the Dodge City Airport Depot, and now we’re stuck in the middle of nowhere in our Sunday best!

KID CUZZOLA

(Stands up slowly, dramatically removes cowboy hat, and holds a wooden spoon like a microphone)

Hold on now, partners. Let me say a few words. When I look at this dusty, stranded posse… I don’t see a broken wagon. I see love. I see family. I see—

SHERIFF

(Interrupting, deadpan)

Kid, save the Best Man speech for Christmas dinner. It’s 90 degrees out here and Barnaby’s melting in that tuxedo vest.

BUCKAROO BARNABY

(Eyes lighting up, pointing off-camera)

Wait a second… is that a watering hole?!

CALAMITY COOKIE

Last one in is a rotten reindeer!

On the count of three, everyone yells “YEEHAW!” and dives onto the couch/floor in slow motion as if jumping into a swimming pool. BUCKAROO BARNABY dramatically whips off their jacket/vest mid-dive, revealing oversized boxer shorts or pajama pants underneath.

SHERIFF

(Popping up from behind the couch, wearing swimming goggles over their cowboy hat)

Still beats waiting for a replacement bus! Merry Christmas, ya filthy outlaws!
